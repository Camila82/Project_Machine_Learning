Reinforcement Learning

Core concepts behind autonomous intelligent decision-making systems.

Reinforcement Learning (RL)

A paradigm where an agent learns to behave in an environment by performing actions and seeing the results in the form of rewards or penalties.

The Intelligent Agent

The entity that makes decisions. It perceives the environment, performs actions, and updates its policy based on the feedback received.

Environment & States

The physical or virtual world the agent inhabits. A "state" is a specific condition or configuration of the environment at a given time.

Key Mechanisms

Exploration vs. Exploitation

The fundamental dilemma in RL. Should the agent try new, unknown actions (exploration) or stick to what it knows has worked before (exploitation)? A balance is required for optimal learning.

Reward Policy

The policy is the agent's strategy—the mapping from states to actions. The "reward" is the numerical signal that tells the agent if its action was desirable or not.
